says nothing about the others.

> **Standing caution.** A green CI run is not evidence of specification approval. A

## A. Technical implementation readiness — PARTIAL

The v0.6.2 normative machinery is implemented once, in the shared core and store, and is
in force for all five adapters: three-state determinacy with N/A purity (BC-1/BC-2,
PR #8/#19), the incurred definition of `immediate` (PR #10/#11 corrections), committed-
evidence provenance and reconciliation precedence (PR #19), the four-class outcome
taxonomy with retry discipline (PR #16/#17), the §6.12 state-verification hook
(PR #18), and the durable write-ahead layer (PR #17) — and, after the reconciliation
round, authorization preservation (PR #24), shared runtime proposal validation
(PR #25), subscription identity binding and unit comparability (PR #26), and the
append-only receipt-evidence model with derived current views (PR #27). The full suite
stands at **719/719 on `main` (`503928d`, post-merge; independently re-verified
Oct 31, 2026 — see §F)** and every rule in the candidate's Appendix B
(75 rule IDs) maps to implementation plus pinning tests in
`conformance/v0.6.2-traceability.md`, per its MUST/MUST NOT audit.

**Evidence honesty (unchanged in kind, updated in numbers).** No real provider-backed
live commit has ever been executed: every commit-path behaviour above is
SIMULATED_PASS at best (mocked transports, in-repo simulators). LIVE_PASS has never
been claimed or achieved for any commit; the only live evidence in the repository
remains the historical read/estimate session. The research suite stands at 11 pass /
7 fail — repro43 1/2, repro44 6/0, repro45 4/2, repro46 0/3 — and all seven failures
are **guard-blocked harness results** at the PR-14 preservation gate: the harnesses
attempt OPERATION-authorized Chargebee-live commits the gate refuses pre-dispatch, so

evidence about those layers (gap G-20).

Why PARTIAL and not PASS: two rules are assessed PARTIAL in the traceability matrix —
snapshot determinacy is emitted but never consumed (V62-DET-08 / gap G-07), and three
adapters' state establishments are adapter-owned equivalents rather than consumers of
the shared hook (V62-SV-02 / gap G-08) — and the inherited conformance gaps G-2/G-5/G-6
remain open (G-09), as do the Finding-7 residuals (G-21 — including the
same-subscription exclusion's implemented (quote_id OR subscription_id) keying, which
§6.16 as reconciled now states honestly). None of these is a known unsafe behaviour; all fail closed. They are
completeness gaps in the implementation/evidence, recorded rather than resolved by
fiat.

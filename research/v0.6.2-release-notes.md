# SCQ v0.6.2 — experimental specification

Release notes for the Git tag `spec-v0.6.2`. These notes are the GitHub Release body for
that tag. Specification: `spec/scq-v0.6.2-spec.md`. Previous specification: SCQ v0.6.1.

SCQ v0.6.2 is published as an **experimental** normative specification. It was
ratified by the maintainer and merged on Oct 31, 2026 (PR #21). Publication
asserts the semantics below as the project's current experimental text. It is
**not** a conformance certification for any implementation, **not** a release
of the reference package (which remains unpublished), and **not** an
authorization to execute subscription changes against any provider — no live
provider-backed commit has ever been executed under this specification.

## What v0.6.2 adds over v0.6.1

- **Fail-closed determinacy (BC-1).** The determinacy gate runs over the
  quote's participating **financial** dimensions (§3.10, §6.13). Before
  dispatch, a quote any of whose participating financial dimensions cannot
  be determined — its value, its provenance, or its comparable units — is
  refused and does not commit. After a commit, an effect on such a dimension
  that cannot be established under §3.11's permitted committed-figure
  provenance (`provider_actual` or contract-justified `defined_equal_exact`)
  is recorded UNVERIFIED (`COMMITTED_UNVERIFIED`), never claimed as an
  established effect. The core does not independently authenticate the
  adapter's provenance classification (V62-EVD-01). The gate proves nothing
  about effects outside the financial dimensions it assesses, nor about tax,
  discount, or conversion outcomes.
- **Candidates are claims, not actuals.** A figure recorded under UNKNOWN
  determinacy is a *candidate* (§3.10): recorded so that later verification
  has the original figures to judge, never treated as the effect that
  occurred. An initial EXACT receipt may establish committed figures by the
  contract-defined `defined_equal_exact` source, subject to the adapter
  trust limitation (§3.11); this is not independent provider authentication.
  Later promotion of a recorded receipt to a verified current view requires
  a separate, independently established post-commit observation (§6.15).
  The recorded receipt itself is never rewritten by later evidence.
- **Honest timing semantics.** `immediate` names the obligation the change
  itself *incurs* at its effective time (§3.8.1) — not an invoice total, not
  an amount presently due, and not cash that has moved; and every committed
  figure carries explicit provenance stating what it is (§3.11). A
  pre-mutation estimate cannot establish a committed actual, and an adapter's
  `provider_actual` label is not by itself independent proof of a provider
  event; the provenance and verification limits remain explicit.
- **Outcome taxonomy and retry discipline.** Four outcome classes with
  INDETERMINATE as a terminal, non-retryable recording; re-dispatch is
  possible only after independently confirmed pre-effect non-execution, under
  identical request, binding, exclusion, expiry, and fingerprint gates
  (§6.16).
- **Authorization preservation (§6.17).** An adapter whose update mechanism
  cannot preserve the authorization scope a quote was issued under must
  refuse before dispatch — on Chargebee's full-replacement update path,
  OPERATION-authorized updates are refused outright.
- **Runtime proposal validation (§6.18).** One shared validator at quote
  creation and commit: guarantee modes are exact, tolerance envelopes are
  shape-checked with own-property reads only, and no code path promotes a
  malformed proposal to a silent default.
- **Identity and unit comparability (§6.19).** Effects are established
  against the subscription the quote was issued for — foreign records are
  refused before dispatch and never used as evidence after it — and amounts
  are compared only within an established unit basis; an unestablishable
  participating currency makes the dimension UNVERIFIED, never silently
  equal.
- **Append-only receipt evidence (§6.15).** The recorded receipt is
  immutable; verification arrives as an append-only evidence trail; the
  "current" receipt view is derived from that trail, never authored, and
  promotion to a verified view requires one coherent observed-state basis.
- **Durable write-ahead layer (§6.16)** with pinned recovery semantics, and a
  provider-neutral state-verification hook (§6.12).

## Governance

v0.6.2 was reconciled against the reference implementation across PRs #23–#27;
its nine contested design dispositions were ratified by the maintainer (four
ratified, one ratified conditionally, four deferred by ratified decision). A
conformance-closure pass followed (issue #29, PRs #35–#42): G-09's inherited
test-path gaps were closed at simulation level; the Chargebee-simulator update
path was migrated to the shared state-verification hook, fixing a
cross-subscription verification defect; a core defect that let forged
`defined_equal_exact` labels reconcile OK was fixed; the seven guard-blocked
research cases were accounted per case; and the Finding-7 durable residuals
were pinned. G-17 provider-mechanism research and a mock-only prototype
followed (PRs #32, #38). At publication the reference suite stands at
**803/803** on Node 20 and Node 22; the research suite stands at **11 pass /
7 fail**, the seven being guard-blocked by design (G-20, below). The full
record is in `research/v0.6.2-release-gaps.md`; conformance mapping is in
`conformance/v0.6.2-traceability.md` (75 rules).

## Known limitations — read before relying on this text

- Three conformance rows are PARTIAL:
  - **V62-DET-08** (G-07): snapshot determinacy (`recurringAmountState`) is
    emitted and labelled honestly, but no enforcement path consumes it — it
    is descriptive only. Closing the row requires a maintainer decision
    (mark the field explicitly informative, or name a future consumer), not
    an implementation change.
  - **V62-EVD-01** (G-01): the core trusts adapter-established EXACT source
    classification and does not independently authenticate provider events —
    the specification states this trust limitation itself (§3.11). The
    record-internal half is closed: a `defined_equal_exact` label contradicted
    by the record's own figures now classifies VIOLATED on every path
    (PR #39). The authentication half is structural and stands.
  - **V62-SV-02** (G-08, narrowed): the Chargebee-simulator UPDATE path now
    consumes the shared state-verification hook (PR #37). Three
    establishments remain adapter-owned — Chargebee-simulator CANCEL, Paddle
    CANCEL, and Zuora — because the hook's contract cannot express an
    established absence of a scheduled cancellation, a `cancelledDate`-bearing
    snapshot, or sequence-derived expectations.
- **Closed since the candidate audit — G-09:** the inherited v0.6.1 test-path
  gaps (TERM_END/EXACT refusal, adapter replay, end-to-end VIOLATED
  reconciliation) are closed at simulation level by direct adapter-level
  evidence (PR #36; test52, test51, test25.8). This is simulation-level
  evidence, not provider-backed validation.
- A disclosed residual: receipts minted by a pre-fix derivation rule with
  degenerate observed-state identities whose digests are not enumerable at
  recovery cannot be re-derived (spec §9, U-14); such identities cannot be
  minted anymore.
- Chargebee-bound OPERATION-authorized updates cannot be executed by the
  reference live adapter at all (G-17): its sole update mechanism is full-list
  replacement (`FULL_REPLACEMENT_ONLY`), no documented conditional write
  exists, and the specification's preservation requirement is enforced there
  as pre-dispatch refusal (`AUTHORIZATION_PRESERVATION_UNENFORCEABLE`), which
  is the intended behaviour; recommendation C (preserve the refusal) stands.
  The G-17 mechanism research and prototype (PR #38, test57) are **mock-only
  research** — not a live Chargebee mutation capability. A controlled
  Chargebee test-site commit remains NO-GO (issue #12, gap G-12).
- Deferred by ratified decision, fail-closed in the interim: TARGET_REPLACEMENT
  semantics (G-14), currency/cadence schema extensions (G-16), safe-integer
  tightening (G-18), strict plain-object envelope enforcement (G-22).
- The research suite's seven failures are guard-blocked, not layer failures
  (G-20): the repro harnesses attempt Chargebee-live OPERATION-authorized
  commits that the authorization-preservation gate (§6.17) refuses before
  dispatch, so those tests demonstrate the refusal and never reach — and say
  nothing about — the identity and receipt-evidence layers behind the guard.
  The probed behaviours are pinned separately by test47, test49, test50, and
  test55 (PR #40, integrated as PR #42), with a per-case accounting in
  `research/g20-guard-blocked-evidence.md`. One question the closure pass
  surfaced remains open (issue #46): when a concurrent write breaks the
  attribution fingerprint bracket, the recorded immediate figure is priced off
  the state the operation began on rather than the validated state — the
  classification stays safe (UNVERIFIED), but whether such a figure should
  record as DETERMINED is undecided.
- Finding-7 residuals remain open (G-21), now executably pinned (PR #41,
  test56): the durable store's dispatch exclusion keys on (quote_id OR
  subscription_id) with no provider/site predicate, so the same subscription
  id on two Chargebee sites mutually blocks (over-broad, availability-side;
  §6.16 states the implemented keying) — provider/site scoping is tracked in
  issue #45; unblocking an INDETERMINATE attempt (`resolveAttempt`) flips only
  an operator-review flag — **the operator's note is not persisted on the
  attempt record, and no structured resolution evidence exists** — tracked as
  issue #43 (P0: persist independently reviewed resolution evidence for
  INDETERMINATE attempts); and the Stripe simulator's EXACT/CANCEL committed
  effect is recorded from the mutation call's return value with no independent
  post-commit re-read — the wrong-state negative is **not constructible**
  through the simulator's public API (it has no misexecution fault), a
  documented non-constructibility tracked in issue #44 rather than coverage.
- Envelope nomenclature (G-19): the bound `maxImmediateCreditDecrease` is
  named and documented as bounding a decrease in immediate *credit*
  magnitude, but it in fact bounds any favorable net movement of the
  immediate dimension — a smaller charge with no credit involved consumes
  the budget exactly as a larger credit does. The mathematics is pinned and
  unchanged; envelope authors should read the bound by its semantics
  (favorable-movement magnitude), not by its name.
- All commit-path evidence behind this specification is simulated or mocked,
  and **no complete independent provider-backed commit validation exists**.
  The evidence classes remain distinct: simulated tests; provider
  documentation; historical live read/quote observations (a Chargebee test
  site — retrieval and quote generation only); and no verified live
  mutations. The error-classification allowlist has never met a real provider
  error surface, and the origin manifest has no external trust anchor.
- This publication is **not a production certification**, carries **no
  provider endorsement**, and makes **no blanket implementation-conformance
  claim**; the three PARTIAL rows above stand as published.
- **No npm reference package is published.** `reference/package.json` remains
  `private: true`, version `0.1.0`, with a description asserting v0.6.1 —
  unchanged by this publication. Package release is a separate gated decision
  (`research/v0.6.2-publication-decision.md` §4.2: NO-GO).
